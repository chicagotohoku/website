CTQA at Tohoku University Open Campus 2026

Overview

  • Date: July 29–30, 2026
  • Location: Tohoku University, Sendai, Japan
  • Type: Open campus exhibit
  • Format: Public outreach

About the Exhibit

Members of the Chicago-Tohoku Quantum Alliance will participate in Tohoku University Open Campus 2026 with interactive outreach activities introducing concepts in quantum science and technology.

Students and researchers from Tohoku University and the University of Chicago will work together to engage visitors through accessible, hands-on activities.
